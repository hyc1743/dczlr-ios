# v0.1.29

Fixes the fatal error in the train scene and restores smooth interaction across Scene 4 (`home_`), Scene 5 (`trai_`), and Scene 6 (`toil_`):

- Removes out-of-range bone animation requests (`clip 18/19`) on the Scene 5 auxiliary models (`sm0X_00.xx + sn0X_00.xa` / `sm0X_01.xx + sn0X_01.xa`) whose pose tracks only cover `clips 1..5`, eliminating the fatal `"Watch auxiliary model tick failed"` error on train scene interaction.
- Fixes same-frame context menu dismissal when left-clicking the right-hand main icons (`P_chara`, `P_fuku`, `P_cam`, `P_sys`).
- Includes `_face.model` in `motionHitIndexAtX:y:width:height:` so clicking or dragging across the character's head/face registers hit regions alongside the body.
- Keeps Scene 4 interactive touch (`clip 5`) looping between drag strokes and on `P_chara` (`M_k001`) selection instead of prematurely chaining to climax (`clip 6`) on mouse release, supporting both held left-drag and post-selection cursor drag across Scene 4, Scene 5, and Scene 6.
- Synchronizes `DCGameAudio` playback state and caches `.pp` archive handles across the main thread delegate and render queue.

The IPA is unsigned and requires the existing external `Documents/game/Data` resources.
